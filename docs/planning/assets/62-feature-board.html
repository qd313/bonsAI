Feature 2★★reading aloud · layout · D-pad

Read aloud becomes a small speaker button

Today Read aloud is a full-width line across the answer, the same shape as Show details sitting right below it — two lines that look like the same control. You asked for a small speaker button on the same row as Helpful and Not really, and for the line to go.

Whatever we pick has to keep four things: the D-pad can still stop on it under the same name, Left and Right move along the row, the icon and what it says flip to Stop while the Deck is talking, and it stays reachable when the thumbs are greyed out — because on an answer you stopped part-way, the D-pad steps over the greyed thumbs entirely.

What to decide: icon alone at the far right, icon with its word, or icon right next to Not really.

Feature 6 · how quiet is quiet enough

The microphone in the Ask box sits at 15 per cent of full strength — very faint. That is fine there, because the Ask box is where you already are. The speaker sits under an answer you may be scrolling past, and if it is too faint nobody will ever find it. Both are drawn below on the real background. Whichever you pick, it goes to full strength the moment the ring lands on it.

Screens lie about faint things. This one is worth settling by eye on the Deck rather than on a monitor, and that is a two-minute check once it is built.
