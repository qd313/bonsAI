The chat sums itself up

What you will see on the Deck when a long chat sums itself up instead of being cleared, as picked on 2026-09-24. Drawn at true size: the plugin column is 300 px wide, the chat's reading area 412 px tall and the dock 157 px. Colours and sizes are copied from the plugin's own styles. Nothing here is built yet.

Press Play on a scene to watch it, or press any step to jump there. A white outline shows where the D-pad is. The options that were not picked are in the first version of this page and, in words, in the plan's decision record.

What was decided

  • The Deck's AI writes the summary, only when the chat outgrows its room.
  • After that the AI gets the summary plus the newest turns word for word, never fewer than the newest two.
  • It sums up even while a game is running. No warning beforehand, and no setting to turn it off.
  • While it works you see one line with a timer.
  • A note under the answer says it happened. On the newest answer, pressing it opens the Session tab.
  • Sum up this chat is the first thing in the Session tab, with the summary under it. No confirm box. Afterwards it reads Sum up again.
  • Stop during the summary stops everything. The chat stays as it was.
  • Each chat keeps its own subject. Checklist ticks stay with the game.
  • Written in your reply language, about 200 words, read-only.
  • A very old, very long chat sums up its newest part, in one wait.
  • The look-back spoiler rating gets its own plan, later.

How long the wait is

Not measured yet, because nothing writes a summary yet. Worked out from speeds already measured on the Deck: reading the chat takes a few seconds, and writing a summary of about 200 words with a game running takes 12–20 s.

So expect roughly 20–30 s, once, each time the chat sums itself up. The questions in between cost about a second more than today.

Measuring it is the plan's first step. If it comes out over a minute with a game running, the build stops and the number comes back to you first.

In the animation every wait runs about four times faster than on the Deck.

Scene 1 · on its own

When the chat outgrows its room

You're deep in a Water Temple chat and ask a follow-up that names nothing: "and what about the boots?" The chat is too long to carry whole, so it sums itself up first, then answers, and a note under the answer says so.

    The pieces, as drawn

    The wait line
    Today's waiting line, spinner and all, reading Summing up the chat so far · 12 s. The seconds count up live. Once the summary is written, today's lines take over: Searching knowledge base…, Writing your answer…
    The note
    One line between the answer and Was this helpful?, in the muted blue of the waiting line, with a small squeeze icon: The chat summed itself up before this answer. Press to read what it kept. A D-pad stop only on the newest answer.
    Pressing the note
    Opens Show details on the Session tab, with the summary on screen.
    On older answers
    The same line as plain text, The chat summed itself up here. Not a D-pad stop. See the still picture below.

    Scene 2 · pressing it yourself

    Sum up this chat, in the Session tab

    Show details, then the Session tab, then one press down to Sum up this chat. The summary gets written right away and appears under the button. No confirm box: nothing is deleted, every turn stays in the chat.

      The pieces, as drawn

      The button
      Clear's own quiet full-width look, moved to the top of the tab. Sum up this chat, then Sum up again once a summary exists. While working: spinner and Summing up · 12 s, nothing else.
      Greyed out
      When the whole chat still fits, or while an answer is being written, with one line under it saying why.
      The summary card
      Drawn from the chip ladder's own panel, with a faint blue edge. Header What the AI remembers and how many turns it covers; the summary as short lines; a footer saying how many newest turns are kept word for word and how many hidden notes were left out.
      Below it
      The turn list and its chips, as today. Clear is gone.
      The tab itself
      Shows whenever the chat has a question in it, not only when some turn attached something extra.
