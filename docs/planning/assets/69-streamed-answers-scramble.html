The numbers this mockup copies from the plugin
  • Decode chips: one letter settles every 42 ms; scrambled letters reshuffle every 55 ms; the block cursor blinks every 450 ms; scramble letters are plain keyboard symbols plus narrow Japanese katakana.
  • Streaming today: the panel asks for new text every 150 ms; the smoothing shows text at a steady rate, fast enough to catch up in about 0.18 s, never slower than 40 letters a second; a finished code box reveals three times faster; when the answer ends, whatever is left appears at once.
  • Answer bubble: 12 px text, line height 1.4, 92% of the chat width, the cyan glow and blinking end cursor while it streams. The waiting chip and spoiler box wording here is approximate.
  • Measured on the Deck, 24 September 2026, with Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor running: the model writes a piece every 50 ms, but the plugin passes text on only in blocks of about 30 pieces, because it waits for 4 KB of the model's output before reading any of it. That is one lump of about 115 letters every 1.5–2 seconds. The panel drew 90 frames a second at rest, about 38 while the model was thinking, and 8–40 while answer text appeared.
